Privacy Policy - Which One Are You?

Last updated: October 2026

Which One Are You? is a fun entertainment app that matches your selfie with an animal, flower, or a playful roots map. Your privacy matters to us.

Photos

Photos you capture or upload are processed only on your device to generate your fun result. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers, shared, or sold. We do not have servers that store your photos.

Data we store

The app stores only your display preferences (light/dark theme and selected category) locally on your device. No account is required and no analytics data is collected by us.

Third parties

The app may download font files from Google Fonts when first opened, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Entertainment only

All matches and roots maps are generated for entertainment purposes only and are not scientific measurements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at our GitHub repository.
